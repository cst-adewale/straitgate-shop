How we build

The first thing your guests touch—and the last thing an intruder gets past.

Clean welds, honest weight

No paper-thin imported sheets or rattling frames. We fabricate with thick-gauge steel, seamless ground joints and balanced proportions that close with a quiet, solid thud.

Treated for Lagos weather

Salt air and heavy rain eat cheap metalwork fast. Every door, gate and chair frame is anti-rust primed and finished to resist peeling, rust and fading year after year.

Workshop to your doorstep

Pick your piece online with clear Naira pricing—no endless back-and-forth with roadside welders—and get it delivered straight to your home, estate or office.
